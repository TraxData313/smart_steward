# Changelog

What changed for players, newest first. Every change lands under **[Unreleased]** the day it is made,
as one short line a player understands (no file names, no internals). At release time the
[Unreleased] lines become the Nexus / Steam change notes, the section is retitled to the new version
and date, and a fresh empty [Unreleased] goes on top.
