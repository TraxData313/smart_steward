# Smart Steward

A *Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord* mod that takes the logistics chores off your hands.

Ride into a town or village and your **Party Steward** lays out, in one table, what the party
needs: food (enough, and varied), pack animals, horses for your footmen, war horses for the
upgrades waiting, prisoners to ransom, loot to sell — and who is sitting in the tavern for hire.
Nudge any row with `[-]` `[+]` (shift ×5, ctrl for all), then **Do it**.

Every number is yours to set — in the game, in MCM, or in a plain commented settings file.

**Status:** in development. The spec is [docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md); progress is on
[TASKS_TODO.md](TASKS_TODO.md).

Game version: Bannerlord v1.4.8. Release: Steam Workshop.
