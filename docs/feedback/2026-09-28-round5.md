## How Claude reads it (the manager's calls — Anton may overturn any of them)

**A Goal column = where the row should END (the Result the steward aims for).** For food and horses it is typed in
directly — a *standing order*; everywhere else it only shows where the steward's rules take the line.

- **Columns:** Item · Market · **Goal** · Mine · Change · Result · Denari · Party · Prisoners · **Land weight** · **Sea weight**
  (Item back at the far left — this order replaces round 4's "Market far left").
- **Weight, not kg:** the game writes weight with NO unit (its Encyclopedia: "Most goods have an Weight rating"; the party
  screen shows bare numbers). So the headers say "Land weight" / "Sea weight" and every "kg" in our texts goes (tooltips,
  "lowest price per kg" → per weight).
- **Troops:** the title line's Goal = the party size limit; Mine = members now, **red when over the limit**; Result as today
  (99/102, red when over). Not editable. Troop lines keep their [-][+]; their Goal cell is empty.
- **Food:** every food row's Goal is a typed box.
  - A goal typed by hand is a **standing order**: it holds in every town, across sessions and game restarts, until its ⟲ gives
    the row back to the Instructions policy. Stored in `settings.json` (a commented "Goals" section, global like the price
    book — never in the save). The autonomous steward obeys it too.
  - The steward's own goals (the policy: days, variety, price book) show plainly; a goal of yours shows marked as yours
    (gold, the ⟲ beside it — the same "yours" mark as touched rows).
  - **The policy fills the rest:** your food goals count toward the days goal first, the steward's rows share what is left.
  - A [-]/[+] click on a food row's Change IS a goal edit (the goal becomes the new Result) — one concept per row, one ⟲.
  - Food title line: Goal = the sum of the rows (not editable).
  - **Below the activation threshold** ("Manage food from 2,000 denari") the steward's goal shows as a grey **dash with a
    star** `-*`, hover: *"Not managed yet: the steward starts on food at 2,000 denari — you have 1,450. Type a goal to order it
    anyway."* **[Claude's call — Anton suggested `0*`]**: a goal of 0 means "sell it all" on the Other and Prisoner lines,
    and here nothing is sold either, so a dash ("no goal yet") says it truer. One-line flip if Anton prefers `0*`.
  - Instructions: an info line on top of the Food group — *"A food whose goal you have not typed in the Suggestion tab follows
    these rules."*
- **Horses:** the role rows (Pack animals, Riding horses, War horses) get the same typed Goal + ⟲ (a riding-horse goal is a
  plain number then — it no longer follows the footmen until ⟲). Noble and lame rows: Goal 0, not editable. The per-type
  rows under a role: empty. Info lines on the horse groups in Instructions, like food.
- **Prisoners:** Goal 0 on a line set to Ransom or Donate; on Keep the goal = Mine (nothing moves). Not editable.
- **Other:** Goal 0 on every line (everything unlocked is sold); a line the settings do not sell shows Goal = Mine.
- **"Apply money cap options for manually set stuff"** — a new Instructions group **Goals you set by hand**, three switches:
  - `ManualGoalsWaitForThresholds` — default **OFF**: your goal acts even below the job's "Manage from" denari (it is your order).
  - `ManualGoalsKeepPurseFloor` — default **ON**: the purse floors (`MinGoldAfterDeal`, the animal floor) hold for your goals too.
  - `ManualGoalsObeyPriceCaps` — default **ON**: the price book's max buy / min sell and the role caps hold for your goals too.
  - The autonomous steward's `AutonomousMinGold` holds whatever these say (the rich player's autonomy never drains the chest).
- A goal the market cannot reach (stock, gold, a cap): Result stays short of the Goal, and a hover on the Result says why.
