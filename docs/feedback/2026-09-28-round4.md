## How Claude reads it (the manager's synthesis — the mockup, PLAN step 19, is where Anton confirms)

**The Suggestion tab becomes one spreadsheet.**
- Columns: Item · Mine · Change `[-] n [+] [⟲]` · Result · Market · **Denari** · **Souls** · **Land kg** · **Sea kg**
  (the old Price/Type columns fold into Denari and the section; the per-unit price moves to a tooltip or the Item cell — mockup decides).
- **Sections** (each: a header, OVERVIEW line(s) always visible, DETAIL rows only when expanded; the fold is remembered — step 18):
  1. **Troops** `99/102` (members after the deal / limit, red when over) — one line per tavern wanderer, one Mercenaries line,
     one **Recruits** line (`[+]` takes the highest tier on offer first; ▸ expands to the per-type rows), one **Your troops** line
     (`[-]` dismisses the lowest tier first, wounded first within a type; `[+]` re-adds in the REVERSE order they were dropped;
     brackets say what: "dropping 2 T1 Imperial Recruit, 1 T2 Vlandian Recruit"; ▸ expands to per-type rows, lowest tier first).
  2. **Food** — overview: `5/6 kinds · avg 10 ± 1 per kind · min 2 Olives · 200 → 214 (≈42 → 45 days)` — no ± on it; detail rows = today's per-food rows.
  3. **Horses** — overview: `Horses 110 / 200 before the herd slows you` (red when over); detail rows = today's role rows (▸ per type as today).
  4. **Prisoners** — overview: `50/60 prisoners · 2 lords · avg T3.5 ± 1.0 · T1–T5`; TWO action lines: **Lords** `[Keep | Ransom | Donate]`
     and **Others** `[Keep | Ransom | Donate]` with the result "+X denari" or "+X influence"; the toggles ARE the settings (remembered);
     detail rows per troop type with tier, lowest first, names open the Encyclopedia.
  5. **Other** (was "Armour & weapons") — overview: denari + weight changes; lines Armour, Melee, Ranged, Shields, and a NEW
     **Other goods** line: every UNLOCKED good that is not food, animal or equipment (coal, jewelry, … — livestock excluded) sold in bulk,
     cheapest first.
  6. **Total** — the last line: sums of Denari, Souls, Land kg, Sea kg over everything above.
- **Header:** `Denari 69,358 » 89,189 (+19,831)` + small `+11.2 influence` when any.
- **Footer = only the weight table:** rows Land / Sea; columns before · change · after · capacity · left · slowdown (the vanilla
  party-speed horse icon, the speed lost to overburden on that terrain). Spent/earned/influence/food/party/horses/herd lines are
  gone from the footer (they live in the header and the sections now).
- **Colours by meaning:** green = good for the player (denari or influence gained, count up where it is a gain), red = cost / loss /
  over a limit. (Round 4 bug: a gain shown red, a cost shown green.)
- The `Click ±1 · Shift ±5 · Ctrl all · names in gold open the Encyclopedia` hint moves to the top of the Instructions tab.
- The word **"denari"** everywhere in the GUI (never "gold").

**Behaviour / settings:**
- **Activation thresholds** — a job starts only when the purse (at planning, before this visit's deal) is at least: Food **2,000**,
  Pack animals **2,000**, Riding mounts **5,000**, War horses **20,000**. Below it the job does nothing (no buy, no sell). "Players
  turn it on with the defaults and are happy; as they get richer the jobs switch on."
- `WarMountsToKeep` default **10**.
- **Food price multipliers of its own:** buy **×2.0**, sell **×0.5** (grain at 10 → the steward pays up to 20). Horses keep 1.2 / 0.8.
- **Prices tab:** items ordered by ascending price inside each group.
- **Prisoners:** tier shown, lowest first, clickable to the Encyclopedia; actions per the two lines above (Donate where the game
  does not allow it → others are ransomed, lords are kept).
- "Souls" = everyone who eats: party members + prisoners.
